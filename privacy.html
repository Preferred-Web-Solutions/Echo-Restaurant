Privacy Policy

What we collect through this site, and how it's used.

What we collect

When you use a form on this site — reservations, catering requests, online ordering, or reporting a website issue — we collect what you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, and any message or details you provide.

We don't use tracking cookies or analytics on this site. Loading the page does load fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of delivering those font files.

How we use it

  • To respond to your reservation, catering request, order, or message
  • To follow up on a reported website issue

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except as needed to operate the site (see below).

Third-party services

Form submissions on this site are delivered to us through Formspree, a third-party form processing service. Formspree receives what you submit in order to route it to us by email. You can read Formspree's own privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and keep basic business records, and no longer than necessary.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by reaching out here.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site changes.
